User Story: ATM Cash Withdrawal

As a bank customer, I want to withdraw cash from my checking account using an ATM.

Business Rules & Constraints:
1. The checking account balance must never go below $0.
2. The maximum cash withdrawal in a single transaction is $500.
3. The customer must have sufficient funds in their account to cover the withdrawal.
4. If a withdrawal succeeds, the account balance is decremented by the withdrawn amount.
5. If a withdrawal fails (e.g. insufficient funds or limit exceeded), the account balance must remain unchanged.
